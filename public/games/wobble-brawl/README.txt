WOBBLE BRAWL
============

An original physics brawler made for ROKI. Every fighter is a floppy ragdoll that tries to stay
on its feet; knock out every enemy on the roof to clear a level. Each level is a little tougher,
with a boss every fifth level. Your best level is saved in this browser.

Controls
- A D or arrow keys: move
- W, up arrow or Space: jump
- Click (or hold the mouse button) or J: punch. Punches aim at your mouse; with J and no mouse
  movement, they aim at the nearest enemy's head.
- Right-click or K: kick
- Esc or P: pause. M: sound on/off.

Fast fists and feet hit harder, and head hits count for more. Big hits stun, and a fighter thrown
into the floor or a wall gets hurt too.

What's here
- index.html, game.js, cover.svg: the game, made for ROKI (characters, art and sounds are all
  original; sounds are generated in the browser).
- matter.min.js: Matter.js 0.20.0, the physics engine by Liam Brummitt and contributors
  (https://github.com/liabru/matter-js), unchanged, under the MIT License in LICENSE-matter-js.txt.

Everything loads from this folder, so it works offline on ROKI.
