WOBBLE BRAWL
============

An original physics brawler made for ROKI. Every fighter is a floppy ragdoll: fights happen in the
air, with fighters lunging at each other, tumbling and getting launched across the roof, and they
only stand up straight once they land. Knock out every enemy on the roof to clear a
level; each level is a little tougher, with a boss every fifth level. Win coins and spend them in
the shop on weapons and skins. Two players can also fight on one keyboard.

Controls (1 player)
- A D or left/right arrows: tap toward an enemy to lunge at them, fist first (with a weapon, it
  swings as you arrive). You can lunge twice more in the air. Hold the key to walk.
- W or up arrow: jump
- S or down arrow: kick
- Space: grab a weapon (your fighter bends down and reaches for the nearest one), press again to
  throw it at whoever is in front of you
- Esc or P: pause. M: sound on/off.

2 players: P1 uses A D W S + Space, P2 uses the arrow keys + Enter. First to win 3 rounds.

Weapons lie around the roof (enemies grab them too). The weapon you pick in the shop is in your
hand at the start of every level. Your coins, skins, weapons and best level are saved in this
browser.

What's here
- index.html, game.js, cover.svg: the game, made for ROKI (characters, weapons, skins, art and
  sounds are all original; sounds are generated in the browser).
- matter.min.js: Matter.js 0.20.0, the physics engine by Liam Brummitt and contributors
  (https://github.com/liabru/matter-js), unchanged, under the MIT License in LICENSE-matter-js.txt.

Everything loads from this folder, so it works offline on ROKI.
